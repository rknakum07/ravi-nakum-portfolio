Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions I hear most.

I work across discovery, UX, interaction design and UI to translate business requirements into clear user journeys and product interfaces. I aim to create experiences that serve users while supporting product goals and technical realities.

I start by clarifying the problem, context and constraints. I then structure information, map workflows, explore interaction models and develop high-fidelity UI in close collaboration with product managers and engineers. I use feedback and available evidence to refine the solution before handoff.

I focus on making the experience understandable and controllable: set clear expectations, communicate what the system is doing, keep users involved in important decisions and offer a useful next step when a result is uncertain or not what they need. Portfolio examples should clearly distinguish shipped AI work from concept explorations.

My product design and collaboration toolkit includes Figma, Adobe XD, Adobe Creative Suite, Zeplin, InVision, Jira and Miro. I also work with website platforms such as Framer, Webflow, WordPress, Wix, Squarespace and Carrd, plus AI-assisted tools including Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT and Cursor.

I align user research and behavioral insights with business objectives, technical feasibility, and product strategy to create experiences that work for both users and businesses.

I work closely with product managers, engineers, and stakeholders to align priorities, communicate design decisions, validate solutions, and ensure the final product matches the intended experience.

I design intuitive web applications, SaaS platforms, dashboards, responsive websites, and complex digital products, focusing on usability, scalability, consistency, and business impact.
